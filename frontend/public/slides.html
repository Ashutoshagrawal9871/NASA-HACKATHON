The Problem: Shifting Seasons & The "Last-Mile" Data Gap

Farmers face unprecedented climate disruptions, but existing digital tools fail them.

🔥 Terminal Heat

Shifting Seasons

Winter-spring growing windows (e.g. Rabi season) are experiencing abnormal heat waves, shortening grain-filling periods.

📂 Unusable Data

The NASA Data Gap

NASA collects terabytes of reanalysis data (MERRA-2), but netCDF files and complex APIs are inaccessible to local agronomists.

⚠️ False Precision

Black-Box Hallucinations

Generic AI apps decode satellite pixels into fake yield forecasts or push unscientific crop recommendations without local soil context.

The Danger: Unverified advice causes smallholder crop failure. Agronomy requires rigorous evidence, not statistical guessing.

System Architecture

The Solution: 4-Tier Transparent Evidence Framework

FieldSignals separates data into distinct, auditable layers rather than merging them into an opaque score.

Evidence Card 1: NASA Meteorological Reanalysis

NASA POWER daily observations (MERRA-2 0.5° × 0.625° grid) computing 30-year seasonal departures in temperature and precipitation.

Evidence Card 2: Modeled Soil Context

ISRIC SoilGrids WMS spatial map layers providing regional topsoil pH context—explicitly marked as modeled geospatial context.

Evidence Card 3: Local Chemical Soil Test

Optional user-reported soil laboratory test inputs (pH, SOC %, lab date) with prominent self-reported provenance warning banners.

Evidence Card 4: Controlled Agronomic Trials

Peer-reviewed multi-year crop sequence trials (Saha et al., 2012) from BHU Varanasi, citing exact tables and measured metrics.

NASA Earth Data

NASA POWER (MERRA-2) & NASA GIBS Integration

Live, edge-computed climate baseline and satellite vegetation backdrop.

🛰️ NASA POWER Climatological Engine

Deterministic mathematical baseline across 29 qualifying years (1991–2019):

  • Leap-day neutral: strictly 181 expected days (Nov 1 – Apr 30).
  • Independent temperature and rainfall completeness thresholds (≥90%).
  • Punjab 2023 Rabi Result: Temp Anomaly -1.18°C, Rain Anomaly +5.17 mm (181/181 valid days).

🌿 NASA GIBS MODIS Visual Layer

Direct WMTS tile integration using MODIS_Terra_L3_NDVI_16Day (250m resolution).

Scientific Guardrail: Rendered strictly as a visual backdrop. FieldSignals prohibits decoding pixel colors to claim unverified numeric field-level NDVI.

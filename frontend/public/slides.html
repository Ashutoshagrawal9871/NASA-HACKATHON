The Agricultural Crisis

The Problem: Shifting Seasons & The "Last-Mile" Data Gap

Farmers worldwide are confronting unprecedented weather volatility, yet existing digital tools fail them.

🌡️ Heat Shifts

Terminal Heat Shocks

Winter-spring growing cycles (such as the Indo-Gangetic Rabi season) experience sudden March temperature spikes, stunting grain-fill and threatening food security.

📁 Data Silos

Inaccessible Space Data

NASA holds multi-terabyte atmospheric models (MERRA-2), but complex netCDF formats and scientific APIs remain completely out of reach for rural agronomists.

⚠️ Overpromising

Black-Box Hallucinations

Generic apps decode satellite image colors into fake numeric yield forecasts or push unverified crop advice without understanding local soil chemistry limits.

The Danger: Bad advice causes irreversible smallholder crop failure. Agricultural adaptation demands verifiable evidence, not generative speculation.

System Architecture

The Solution: 4-Tier Transparent Evidence Framework

FieldSignals separates distinct data types into verifiable layers instead of blending them into an opaque score.

Evidence Card 1: NASA Reanalysis

Daily meteorological data from NASA POWER (0.5° × 0.625° grid) computing 30-year climate departures in temperature and precipitation.

Evidence Card 2: Modeled Soil Context

ISRIC SoilGrids spatial WMS layer providing regional topsoil pH context—explicitly marked as modeled geospatial reference.

Evidence Card 3: Chemical Soil Test

User-reported laboratory soil test inputs (pH, Organic Carbon %, lab name) with prominent self-reported provenance warning banners.

Evidence Card 4: Agronomic Field Trials

Peer-reviewed 3-year randomized crop sequence trials from BHU Varanasi (Saha et al., 2012), citing exact tables, yields, and carbon metrics.

Zero-Hallucination Guarantee: All summaries and anomaly calculations are 100% deterministic, reproducible, and mathematically auditable.

NASA Earth Data

NASA POWER (MERRA-2) & NASA GIBS Integration

Live, serverless climatological engine computing multi-decade anomalies in real time.

🛰️ NASA POWER Baseline Engine

Deterministic mathematical baseline calculated across 29 qualifying years (1991–2019):

  • Leap-day neutral: strictly 181 expected days (Nov 1 – Apr 30).
  • Independent temperature & precipitation coverage thresholds (≥90%).
  • Punjab 2023 Rabi Result: Temp Anomaly -1.18°C, Rain Anomaly +5.17 mm (181/181 valid days recorded).

🌿 NASA GIBS MODIS Visual Layer

Integrated 250m resolution satellite vegetation backdrop via MODIS_Terra_L3_NDVI_16Day (EPSG:3857).

Scientific Guardrail: Displayed strictly as a visual backdrop. FieldSignals actively prohibits decoding RGB tile colors to claim false numeric NDVI.
API Performance: Sub-second serverless execution on Vercel with automated upstream timeout resilience and retry safety.
